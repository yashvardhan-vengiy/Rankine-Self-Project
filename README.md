Reheat Rankine Cycle Simulation & Thermodynamic Analysis | Aspen Plus (Self Project)

•	Designed and simulated a reheat Rankine power cycle in Aspen Plus using a once-through flowsheet, integrating pump, preheater, boiler, superheater, two-stage turbine expansion, intermediate reheater, and condenser under 75 bar maximum pressure and 550°C maximum temperature constraints.

•	Constructed the complete T–s cycle diagram from Aspen Plus thermodynamic state data, tracking phase transitions and entropy/temperature changes from 125°C, 2.32 bar condensation through high-pressure steam generation, turbine expansion, reheat, and final condensation.

•	Performed component-level energy analysis across the pump, heat-addition units, turbines, reheater, and condenser, obtaining approximately 38% cycle thermal efficiency from the simulated stream enthalpy balance.
